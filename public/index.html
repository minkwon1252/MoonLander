How a round works

Each round has three layers. Domestic: every team privately decides one policy card. International: each team may make one move toward another (cooperate, restrict, covertly acquire…). Global: a world event hits everyone after choices are locked in. Then stats update and the round is scored. The winner is not whoever has the highest tech number — it is whoever builds durable, legitimate, well-defended technological leadership.
